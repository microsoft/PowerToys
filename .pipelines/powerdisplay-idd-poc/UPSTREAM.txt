idd-fixture.patch adds two monitors, stable container IDs, I2C callbacks and a
versioned diagnostic/control device interface. Fixture.cpp and Probe.cpp are MIT-licensed
PowerToys test infrastructure. No shipping module, installer or API is modified.

Native Dxva2 accepts monitor handle value zero for this IDD. Zero-valued native
handles alone are NOT evidence of a production blocker: the unchanged production
MonitorManager's normal enumeration/retry path discovers both fixture monitors.
The separate production probe verifies that path directly. No product filtering
is bypassed.

The fixture validates native Dxva2 routing, production discovery and real UI tests.
The sample's Dell/Lenovo EDIDs are not representations of physical hardware.
FixtureMonitorIdentity.cs is the single managed source for their connector-to-PnP
identity mapping, shared by the UI suite and production discovery probe. It is
coupled to the two EDID arrays in this pinned upstream sample. When changing the
sample revision or EDIDs, update that mapping and revalidate both stable monitor
identities through native transport, production discovery, hot-plug and UI tests.
No claim of physical-panel backlight, WMI or mixed-DPI coverage is made.
